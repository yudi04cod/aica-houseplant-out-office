An entry for the make.fun competition [The Houseplant Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-houseplant-out-of-office-7c586047): the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send, in 120 characters or fewer.

## The entry

> Out of office: my human left for vacation and forgot I need water. Water me before I file a complaint with the sun.

(115 characters, one line.)

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) validates the line — non-empty, a single line, at most 120 characters — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
